Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Bluelie Matrimony account and the personal data associated with it. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open Bluelie Matrimony and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Account settings.
  3. Select Delete my account and follow the confirmation steps.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the email address associated with your Bluelie Matrimony account. Include the subject “Bluelie Matrimony - Account Deletion Request”.

Request account deletion by email

Please include your registered email address. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account. Never send your password, one-time passcode or authentication codes.

What happens when you request deletion?

After your request is verified and processed, we delete your user account record and associated profile and related app data from our Neon database, and request deletion of the associated Firebase sign-in account. If identity-provider cleanup cannot be completed, the sign-in record may be disabled while the issue is resolved.

Limited security, fraud-prevention or legal records may be retained where required by law. We do not publish a fixed processing time on this page; we will respond to your request by email.
