Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bluelie Matrimony ("Bluelie", "we", "us" or "the Platform") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Privacy-first discovery
Your profile remains hidden while it is a draft or awaiting review. Discovery previews are limited and do not show your name, email address, phone number or exact home address.

1. Who can use Bluelie

Bluelie Matrimony is intended only for adults aged 18 years or above. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to register or create a matrimonial profile.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may process:

  • Account information: email address, Firebase user identifier, account name and sign-in provider.
  • Matrimonial profile information: name, age, profile created for, looking for, city, caste/community, household food preference, date of birth, birth time, birth place, height, marital status, education, profession, family type, family details, introduction and partner preferences.
  • Safety and service information: reports, blocks, moderation decisions, notifications and related audit records.
  • Technical information: limited technical and security logs needed to operate and protect the Platform.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, save profile drafts, review submitted profiles, show limited previews of approved profiles, apply discovery filters, operate blocking and reporting, send in-app moderation notices, secure the service and comply with applicable law.

4. What other members can see

Draft and pending-review profiles are not shown in discovery. Approved active profiles may appear as limited previews showing fields such as age, city and profession. Discovery does not return your name, biography, caste/community, household food preference, family details, email address, phone number or exact address. Full profile details are currently locked. The app profile form does not ask for a phone number or exact home address.

5. Moderation, reports and blocking

Authorised moderators may access profile information and report details to review profiles and safety complaints. Blocking hides profiles from discovery in both directions. Administrator access is restricted by server-side authorisation.

6. Service providers

The current service uses Google Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Render for backend hosting and Neon for managed PostgreSQL database services. These providers process information as needed to operate the Platform and are subject to their own terms and privacy notices. Information may be processed in locations where their infrastructure operates.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers described above, authorised moderators, other members only to the limited extent described in this policy, or authorities where required by law. Account email addresses, phone numbers and exact home addresses are not published in discovery previews.

8. Security

We use safeguards including authenticated API access, server-side access checks, restricted administrator permissions, private/no-store API response headers and request rate limits. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Retention and deletion

Profile and account information is retained while your account is active. You can request account deletion from Account settings → Delete my account, or email darshled001@gmail.com with the subject “Bluelie Matrimony - Account Deletion Request”. Account deletion removes your user record and associated profile and related app data from the Neon database, and Firebase sign-in account deletion is also requested. If identity-provider cleanup fails, a sign-in record may remain disabled temporarily; contact us if this occurs. Limited security or legal records may be retained where required by law. Specific retention periods will be published after they are finalised.

10. Your choices and requests

The current profile form requires the listed profile details to be completed before submission for review, including date of birth, birth time, birth place, current city, caste/community, household food preference, education, family details and partner preferences. Profile-photo upload is planned for a later update and is not active or required in the current version. You may contact us for access or correction requests.

11. Notifications

The app currently provides certain in-app profile moderation and account notices. Push notifications are not currently configured.

12. Payments, advertising and analytics

Paid membership, Google Play Billing and payment entitlement verification are not currently enabled. The current version does not describe an advertising SDK or analytics service as active. This policy will be updated before such features are introduced.

13. Policy changes

We may update this policy as the Platform, service providers or applicable law changes. The latest version and effective date will be made available on this page and in the app. We will provide notice and obtain consent where required.
